Customer-facing reusable support snippet

Understanding ChatGPT Free Trials and Usage Limits

Derived from review complaints about plan restrictions and a reported free-trial payment failure. This does not assert that the customer was incorrectly charged.

  • Eligibility: Promotional trials may have eligibility requirements and specific terms. Availability can vary by offer.
  • Payment method: Some promotions require a valid payment method before the trial begins.
  • Renewal: A trial may automatically renew as a paid subscription at its end unless cancelled beforehand, according to the offer terms.
  • Feature limits: Usage limits can differ by plan, model, and feature. File uploads, image generation, and voice can have separate limits.
  • Payment errors: If a trial payment is declined, review the displayed error and contact the relevant payment provider or OpenAI Support. A review alone cannot establish why it failed.
  • Before confirming: Check the trial duration, eligibility, payment requirements, renewal terms, and cancellation instructions shown for your offer.
